About this report

This is a market-wide snapshot, not an audit of a single business. We picked one competitive local market — dance and ballroom instruction in the Fort Worth, TX area — and ran the same measurement against every studio that competes for it: an automated site-quality grade across seven categories, where each studio shows up in Google's local results, and 6 live checks asking three AI engines who they'd recommend for two real buyer queries. Every studio named below is graded on identical criteria, on the same day, with no advance notice.

The point isn't to single any one studio out — it's to show what an AI-search-era competitive landscape actually looks like once you measure it instead of guessing at it. If your studio is one of the 12 below, this is where you stand today.

What the field shows

  • The top of the field is tight — and unfinished. Dance With Me Fort Worth (83) and Fred Astaire Southlake (82) lead, with three studios tied at 80 right behind. Every one of the top five still carries at least one D or F category.
  • No studio earns an A for AI readiness. The field averages 66 (D) on the category that decides whether an AI engine can read, trust and quote a site; the best score in the market is 85.
  • 11 of 12 have no FAQ or Q&A structured data, and 10 of 12 have no llms.txt file — the two cheapest AI-answer signals available. No studio in the market has both.
  • All 12 studios have images without alt text, from 1 of 229 images to 202 of 417 — easy points every site in the field is leaving on the table.
  • Seven of the 12 are franchise locations (three Fred Astaire, two Arthur Murray, two Dance With Me). The Fred Astaire and Dance With Me locations live on their brands' national websites, so part of each score reflects the corporate template rather than a local decision.
  • One site in the field still isn't served over HTTPS — a critical flag browsers show visitors before they see a single class schedule.
  • The gap between #1 and #12 is 26 points on identical criteria. Nothing in the scorecard requires a rebuild or a rebrand to close it — just the work.

What separates the winners from the rest

  • Off-site proof, not just on-site polish. In its two answers, ChatGPT cited third-party review and directory sites (threebestrated.com, circularstudios.com, bestprosintown.com, ballroomdancedirectory.com, reviews.birdeye.com, distinguishedteaching.com, danceus.org) — not a single studio's own website. The studios named most are the ones those sources already describe clearly.
  • Being unmistakably “in Fort Worth.” Every AI mention went to a studio inside the city, and no studio from outside Fort Worth ranks higher than #6 in any Fort Worth map search. Studios that serve Fort Worth from nearby towns need pages, profiles and listings that say so in the words people search.
  • A complete, specific site. The strongest sites have no missing H1s or meta descriptions, alt text on their images, and schema that describes what the studio actually is. No studio in this field has all of that yet.
  • Content matched to how people actually ask. The broad question and the ballroom question pull from different result sets — a studio needs to answer both.

Methodology & notes

Field (12 studios): dancewithmeusa.com/studio/fort-worth-tx-dance-studio, fredastaire.com/southlake, arthurmurraygrapevine.com, dancewithmeusa.com/studio/southlake-texas-dance-studio, thedanceplacetexas.com, voltadancestudio.com, fredastaire.com/ft-worth, fwdance.com, fredastaire.com/colleyville, dailydanceservices.com, rldancestudio.com, dancemakersoftexas.com. Chosen from live Google searches on 29 Sep 2026: every adult ballroom, Latin or partner-dance studio with a working website that appeared in Google's local results for Fort Worth dance searches. Left out: dance halls and social clubs that offer lessons, instructors without a local studio, youth and recital studios, and Dallas-side studios.

Site audit: the same SiteTradeIn engine run for every studio on 29 Sep 2026, up to six pages per site, seven categories. Franchise locations that live on a national website (Fred Astaire, Dance With Me) share that site's robots.txt, llms.txt, sitemap and templates.

AI visibility: 6 live checks — ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI, each asked “What are the best dance studios in Fort Worth, TX?” and “What are the best ballroom dance studios in Fort Worth, TX?” with a request for a top-five numbered list. Rankings are taken from each engine's own list; presence was confirmed by reading the raw response text, and every franchise mention was matched to its location by hand. The two Google AI checks hit a timeout and a temporary capacity error on the first attempt and were re-run successfully a few minutes later.

Google: local (Places) and first-page organic results captured in a live browser session on 29 Sep 2026 for “ballroom dance lessons fort worth tx”; “dance lessons fort worth tx”; “salsa lessons fort worth tx”; “wedding dance lessons fort worth tx”; “two step lessons fort worth tx”; “ballroom dance lessons arlington tx”, plus the organic searches “best dance studios in fort worth tx” and “ballroom dance studio fort worth”. Each query named the city; results vary by searcher location and over time.

Independence. This report is a market-wide measurement, not commissioned by or customized for any single studio named in it.

Limits. AI answers and search results vary between runs and over time; these are a point-in-time sample, not a fixed ranking.
